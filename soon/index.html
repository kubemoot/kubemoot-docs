Kubemoot is an emerging Kubernetes controller for agentic consensus: a moot of small, open models, running on your own hardware, that deliberate at a round table and settle on an answer together. No single model holds the gavel.

Crews, agents, and their rules are Custom Resources. You apply a crew the way you apply a Deployment.
